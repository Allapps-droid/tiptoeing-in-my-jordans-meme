# Photo and format guide

## Give each photo one clear role

| Photo | Role in the finished video |
| --- | --- |
| First photo | The foreground dancer who tiptoes, makes the hush gesture, and dances. |
| Optional second photo | The person seated in the straw and appearing in the reaction shots. |

With one photo, the original seated character stays in the scene.

## Choose a clear portrait

A front-facing portrait with even lighting gives the face the clearest reference. Keep the whole face visible and choose one subject per photo. Strong shadows, sunglasses, and heavy filters can obscure the features that distinguish the subject.

## Choose the frame before generating

| Format | Shape | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16:9 landscape | Wide | A wider view of the barn, desktop viewing, and landscape posts. |
| 9:16 portrait | Vertical | Phone feeds, Reels, Shorts, and TikTok. |

Both formats use the full 12-second scene. The finished file is an MP4 with the original beat and no watermark.

## Add a caption when you share

Match a short caption to the retreat, the hush, or the dance. Use your social app or usual editor to add post text; the generator delivers the underlying meme video.

## Find the finished result

Generation continues on the server if you leave the page. Return to My Videos in your account to play or download the result.
